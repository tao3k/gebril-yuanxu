* Research question

Human-Agent collaboration is a primary motivation: see [[file:../../docs/10-19-design/10-configuration/10.06-human-agent-module-authoring.org][the authoring study]].
Assess both ordinary configuration and assisted creation of new abstractions.
Historical authoring-cost assumptions are not binding framework constraints, while
their semantic results still need to be addressed. [[file:../../docs/10-19-design/10-configuration/10.05-guestbook-context-and-contribution-design.org][Guestbook]] supplies the shared
case without freezing a Nix-style public args interface.

Can ordinary configuration stay as small as assigning values while maintainers
independently publish thousands of reusable, contract-bearing objects? The goal
is to separate package identity, selected dependencies, effective configuration,
Contract admission and explicit effects, while retaining Scheme functions and
native POO refinement as extension mechanisms.

The organization of research must support independent review. Read the
[[file:../../docs/20-29-ecosystem-research/README.org][research library]] and its [[file:../../docs/30-39-research-foundations/30-method/30.01-evidence-protocol.org][evidence protocol]]. Each platform owns its own
configuration/source observations, issue records, reflection and conclusions.
The previous mixed platform sections have been moved into those dossiers;
this agenda does not repeat them or substitute for their further research.

* Cross-platform synthesis and outstanding work

[[file:08-infrastructure-platforms-and-design-gates.org][Architecture and design gates]] owns comparisons across these subjects.
[[file:../../docs/10-19-design/10-configuration/10.02-review-controls.org][Review controls]] and [[file:../../docs/10-19-design/10-configuration/10.03-outcome-states.org][outcome-state boundaries]] retain shared questions without

[[file:../../docs/10-19-design/10-configuration/10.04-module-system-design-questions.org][The module-system design study]] compares the public interface, contextual
instantiation, contribution semantics, dependency granularity and phase ownership.
The object of design is the Module System; Scheme/POO supplies mechanisms and does
not by itself answer these architectural questions. Each research article includes
an Issues and Engineering Limitations section with qualified original references.

A Module/args design is still a proposal in
[[file:06-directory-windows-and-context-args.org][directory windows and contextual arguments]]. No syntax or implementation is
frozen by the reading agenda. A small Ops/checks/release example cannot establish
large-ecosystem performance or correctness of AWS/Kubernetes consumers.

* Issues and Engineering Limitations

The [[file:../../docs/30-39-research-foundations/30-method/30.01-evidence-protocol.org][research protocol]] defines reference marks and version/status obligations.
Original reports belong to each independent dossier; the agenda makes no new
CONFIRMED-DEFECT or runtime claim. The [[file:../../docs/10-19-design/10-configuration/10.04-module-system-design-questions.org][module-system study]] links architectural
questions back to those qualified records.

* Research status

The current dossiers preserve initial reading and explicit research gaps. Their
creation is an organizational correction, not completion of a platform study.
Substantive source analysis, complete cases, negative controls and independent
conclusions must precede claims that Scheme/POO improves on a platform.
