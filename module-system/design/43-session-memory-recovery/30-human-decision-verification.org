* Focused evidence

[[file:29-human-decision-source-and-admission.org][The Human source/admission protocol]] passed eleven focused Quint Cases:
safe one-winner/current-at-publication invariants, two authorized proposals,
successful publication and pending withdrawal reachability, plus actual
counterexamples for forged/foreign sources, withdrawn authorization replay,
decision expiry, two winners, retired source and wrong target. Parser errors,
timeouts and nonzero exits without TLC counterexamples are not counted as controls.

ten theorems/eight guards using LeanPOO Focus: unissued/foreign/stale-source,
wrong-scope, expired, withdrawn, retired and consumed refusal; one publication;
and preservation of an already committed result under later withdrawal. These
are conditional pure laws, not authentication of a remote Human or a verified
refinement of arbitrary Scheme execution. Dependency warnings remain visible.

previous 219. Source forgery/copy, wrong credentials, duplicate event identity,

withdrawal and old authorization replay, withdrawal inside the policy callback,
expired decision with unexpired recovery authorization, and clock rollback all
fail closed. Positive controls establish actual authenticated decision and
authorization before withdrawal/expiry. Existing fresh recovery/bootstrap/review
and retained-knowledge controls continue to pass.

The independent Module competition uses two real Gerbil threads, one shared
predecessor/authenticated decision, two prepared proposals and two actual Core
authorizations. Both threads join within two seconds; exactly one publishes,
the generation advances once and the decision is consumed. Replay fails;
withdrawal after publication preserves the installed successor. It is not a
cross-thread Assurance effect Host test or a durable concurrency claim.
