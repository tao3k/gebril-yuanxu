* Positive and negative controls

- Shared current support is a candidate, not a consumer review or grant.
- Session A's issued review cannot admit B; A's configured mapping cannot review B.
- B's explicit configured mapping and its own fresh review admit B.
- Source-cut, scope and Governance revision substitution stale the review.
- Blocked Governance refuses consumption and review issuance.
- Copied seal and grant presentations fail private Host issuance checks.
- Review expiry reopens the obligation while domain support remains current.
- Permission revocation preserves candidate evidence but refuses admission.
- ASCENT withdraws the admitted relation and reports revalidation after revocation.
- A new grant does not revive the old grant or refresh an old review.
- Current new grant plus explicit fresh review admits the same consumption scope.
- Effect authorization remains false; delivery calls and service effects are not invoked.

Review-clock and Host-clock controls are independent monotonic inputs so review
expiry can be isolated while Host evidence remains current. This does not prove
cross-clock synchronization or a universal Context decay policy.
