* Successor descriptor admission and same-domain rotation

** Successor-side admission requirement

A content-governed registry transition MUST govern both sides of the transition.
The predecessor side requires exact preservation or authority-issued retirement.
The successor side requires exact pre-existence or authority-issued admission.

Admission evidence MUST bind the descriptor to the canonical successor registry
claim and the successor generation.  When a successor descriptor reuses a
predecessor descriptor's =domainIdentity= with different authority or semantic
identity, admission alone is insufficient: every predecessor descriptor with
that domain identity MUST also carry authority-issued retirement evidence.
Thus a genuinely new domain can be admitted without inventing a predecessor,
while a same-domain semantic rotation has an explicit two-sided disposition.

** Vacuous-predecessor injection counterexample

=retirementOnlyTransitionPermitsUnissuedSuccessorInjection= constructs an empty
predecessor registry and a singleton successor registry.  Identity and
generation transition rules hold, while every predecessor disposition
obligation is vacuous.  The retirement-only contract therefore accepts a
successor descriptor that was neither previously registered nor authority
issued.

This counterexample proves that predecessor preservation-or-retirement cannot
own successor admission.  A separate successor-indexed obligation is required.

** Successor admission Typst model

#+begin_export typst
$
  "SuccessorDisposition"(d, p, n) :=
    "registered"(p."registry", d)
    or "AuthorityIssuedAdmission"(
      d,
      "RegistryClaim"(n."root"),
      n."generation",
    )

  "SameDomainRotation"(d_old, d_new, p, n) :=
    d_old."domainIdentity" = d_new."domainIdentity"
    and d_old != d_new
    and "AuthorityIssuedRetirement"(d_old, p, n)
    and "AuthorityIssuedAdmission"(d_new, p, n)

  "ContentGovernedTransition"(p, n) :=
    "ValidIdentityTransition"(p, n)
    and forall d in p."registry":
      "registered"(n."registry", d)
      or "AuthorityIssuedRetirement"(d, p, n)
    and forall d in n."registry": "SuccessorDisposition"(d, p, n)
$
#+end_export

** Successor admission decision

- Keep =RetirementOnlyContentGovernedRegistryTrustRootTransition= only as the
  named weak contract that owns the vacuous-predecessor counterexample.
- Add =AuthorityValidatedDescriptorAdmissionEvidence= to bind successor
  registration, the canonical successor trust claim, successor generation, and
  retirement of every predecessor descriptor sharing the admitted domain
  identity.
- Add =DescriptorAdmissionAuthority= and
  =AuthorityIssuedDescriptorAdmissionEvidence=.  Lean models the semantic
  =issued= relation; executable signature and commitment validation remain at
  the registry acceptance authority.
- Strengthen =ContentGovernedRegistryTrustRootTransition= with an independent
  =successorDisposition= obligation.  Exact pre-existence and authority-issued
  admission are the only successor branches.
- Prove that an unissued successor injection cannot close the strengthened
  transition, and that a distinct same-domain successor necessarily exposes an
  authority-issued retirement witness for its predecessor.

** Successor admission checklist

- [X] Construct a retirement-only transition with a vacuous predecessor and an
  unissued successor descriptor.
- [X] Bind descriptor admission to successor registration, canonical registry
  claim, successor generation, and an explicit authority issuance relation.
- [X] Require preservation-or-issued-admission for every successor descriptor.
- [X] Require issued predecessor retirement for distinct same-domain rotation.
- [X] Prove unissued successor injection cannot close the strengthened
  transition.
