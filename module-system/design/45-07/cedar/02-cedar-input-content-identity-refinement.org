* Cedar input content-identity refinement

** Input-identity requirement

An authority-bound node containing an exact grant and an exact admitted Cedar
evidence value does not, by containment alone, prove that the grant's four
subject digests identify the evidence's request, entities, and policies.

Final admission MUST use a versioned content-identity authority that projects:

- =Cedar.Spec.Request -> requestDigest=;
- =Cedar.Spec.Entities -> entityStoreDigest=;
- =Cedar.Spec.Policies -> policySetDigest=;
- the complete request/entities/policies tuple to =bundleDigest=.

The projection is bound to the selected lifecycle evidence's authority identity
and to semantic identity/generation fields carried by the authority-validated
grant binding.

** Digest mismatch and collision boundary

=weakAcceptedBindingAllowsCrossSubjectCedarInputs= constructs one accepted pair
whose request, entity-store, and policy identities all differ from the grant
subject.  Merely placing both values in one binding therefore proves nothing
about their content identity.

The contract deliberately does *not* claim that a finite cryptographic digest
is globally injective.  Instead, each authority proves uniqueness only over the
request/entity/policy/snapshot values admitted by its exact generation.  This is
the mathematical boundary needed for replay-safe equality without asserting an
impossible collision-free universe.

#+begin_export typst
$
  I_(closed)(a, s, x) :=
    a.authority = x.authority
    and a.semantic-id = x.semantic-id
    and a.generation = x.generation
    and a."admitted"(x.request, x.entities, x.policies)
    and s.request-digest = a.request-digest(x.request)
    and s.entity-digest = a.entity-digest(x.entities)
    and s.policy-digest = a.policy-digest(x.policies)
    and s.bundle-digest = a.bundle-digest(x.request, x.entities, x.policies)
$

$
  "UniqueOnAdmitted"(a, d) :=
    forall u, v in a."admitted": d(u) = d(v) => u = v
$
#+end_export

** Input-identity decision

- =CedarInputContentIdentityAuthority= is the formal owner of input projection,
  admission, semantic identity, and generation.
- =CompositionCedarInputIdentityRefinementClosed= consumes the exact grant and
  full-lifecycle evidence already paired by the authority-bound node; it does
  not introduce a second copy of either value.
- The full-lifecycle grant binding now carries the identity authority's semantic
  identity and generation so an old projection cannot be replayed.
- Uniqueness is scoped to admitted inputs.  Global cryptographic hash
  injectivity is neither required nor claimed.
- This contract is runtime policy-input identity.  It is not a replacement for
  =gerbil.pkg=, Gerbil-Bazel, or package/source dependency identity.
