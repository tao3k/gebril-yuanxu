* Recovery-authority registry-claim binding

** Recovery-authority identity requirement

The durable content-aware recovery authority MUST bind its issuer identity to
the canonical =PublicationDomainRegistryTrustClaim= selected by the governed
lineage at the durable committed position.  Retaining an =issued= predicate and
durable checkpoint positions does not identify which registry authority or
registry semantics authorized the recovery.

Because registry authority and registry semantics are stable across the
governed lineage, the same validated claim MUST also equal the canonical claim
at the recovered checkpoint.  Replica provenance origins expose this equality
directly and reject either identity mismatch.

** Claim-unbound recovery-authority counterexample

=RegistryClaimUnboundDurableContentAwareRecoveryAuthority= preserves the former
position-only authority shape.  Given any position evidence already issued by
its position authority,
=claimUnboundRecoveryAuthorityPermitsRegistryClaimSubstitution= constructs an
authority that issues the content-aware evidence while an independently chosen
registry claim carries different authority and semantic identities.

The recovery positions remain durable and valid in this counterexample.  The
failure is specifically that their issuer is not identified by the registry
trust root.

** Recovery-authority claim decision

- Preserve
  =RegistryClaimUnboundDurableContentAwareRecoveryAuthority= as the weak model
  and metadata-substitution counterexample owner.
- Strengthen =DurableContentAwareRegistryCheckpointRecoveryAuthority= with
  =validatedRegistryClaim= and =claimMatchesDurableRoot=.  Reuse the existing
  canonical registry claim; do not introduce another authority identity type.
- Derive registry authority and registry semantic identity equality at the
  durable root from exact claim equality.
- Derive the same equalities at the recovered root from governed-lineage
  authority/semantic stability.
- Project both equalities through =AuthorityIssuedReplicaRecoveryOrigin= and
  prove that either mismatch prevents the provenance origin from closing.

** Recovery-authority claim checklist

- [X] Distinguish position issuance from registry-authority identity binding.
- [X] Construct an issued position recovery paired with a substituted registry
  claim.
- [X] Bind safe recovery authority to the canonical durable-root claim.
- [X] Prove durable-root registry authority and semantics equality.
- [X] Propagate those equalities to the recovered root through lineage
  stability.
- [X] Expose the bindings at the authority-issued replica origin.
- [X] Prove registry authority or semantic mismatch cannot close the origin.
