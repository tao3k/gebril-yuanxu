* Authority-provenance recovered-head floor

** Covered-origin floor requirement

An authority-provenance summary that claims to cover a recovery origin MUST
place its =minimumPosition= at or after both that origin's committed position
and its authority-validated recovered position.  A committed-position bound
alone proves that descriptor obligations have a legal starting point; it does
not prove that the summary includes the recovered head already accepted by the
origin's recovery authority.

Every provenance-preserving join MUST retain this recovered-head floor for each
left and right origin.  Consequently the N-way join cannot regress beneath any
covered origin's validated recovery while still claiming that origin as
covered.

** Commit-bound summary counterexample

=commitBoundProvenanceSummaryPermitsRecoveredHeadRegression= retains the old
summary shape as an explicit weak contract.  Given an authority-issued origin
with =committedPosition < recoveredPosition= and an empty committed registry,
it constructs a summary that covers the origin while setting
=minimumPosition = committedPosition=.  All old fields close, yet the summary
minimum remains below the validated recovered head.

The counterexample proves that authority identity and committed descriptor
disposition do not imply recovered-head coverage.

** Recovered-head floor Typst model

#+begin_export typst
$
  "CoveredOrigin"(s, o) implies
    o."committedPosition" <= s."minimumPosition"
    and o."recoveredPosition" <= s."minimumPosition"

  "Join"(l, r)."minimumPosition" :=
    max(l."minimumPosition", r."minimumPosition")

  "CoveredOrigin"("Join"(l, r), o) implies
    o."recoveredPosition" <= "Join"(l, r)."minimumPosition"
$
#+end_export

** Recovered-head floor decision

- Preserve =CommitBoundAuthorityProvenanceReplicaRegistryCheckpointSummary= as
  the named weak counterexample owner.
- Strengthen the existing v1
  =AuthorityProvenanceReplicaRegistryCheckpointSummary= with
  =recoveredHeadCovered=.  Do not introduce a parallel v2 summary.
- Make =authorityProvenanceReplicaSummaryOfRecovery= prove the recovered bound
  by its exact =positionMatchesRecovery= equality.
- Make =joinAuthorityProvenanceReplicaRegistryCheckpointSummary= propagate the
  left or right recovered bound through the corresponding =Nat.max= branch.
- Prove a covered summary cannot be below an origin's recovered head, and expose
  explicit left/right join theorems for N-way composition.
- Derive recovered-only descriptor admission from any covered origin and the
  summary minimum.  The summary's recovered-head floor and the governed
  lineage's successor obligations are therefore available together.
