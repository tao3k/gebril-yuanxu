* Registry trust-claim authority binding

** Registry trust-claim requirement

The publication-domain registry authority identity and registry semantic
identity MUST participate in admission as an exact acceptance-authority-
validated claim.  Merely carrying those fields in
=PublicationDomainRegistryTrustRoot=, while indexing checkpoint evidence by its
=registry= field, does not prove that the metadata selected for this admission
is the canonical metadata of that root.

Registry authority identity and registry semantic identity are registry-level
identities.  They MUST remain distinct from publication-domain descriptor
identity and Cedar input content identity.

** Registry metadata-substitution counterexample

The lineage model already keeps registry authority and semantics stable across
valid transitions.  However, the earlier
=TrustRootBoundCheckpointWatermarkEvidence= wrapper consumes only
=trustRoot.registry=.  The theorem
=trustRootBoundEvidencePermitsRegistryMetadataSubstitution= therefore constructs
two roots backed by the same registry and registered evidence while both
registry authority identity and registry semantic identity differ.

This demonstrates a first-admission gap, not a lineage rollback: stable lineage
cannot repair metadata that was never authority-bound at the admission edge.

** Registry trust-claim Typst model

#+begin_export typst
$
  "RegistryClaim"(r) :=
    (r."registry-authority-id", r."registry-semantic-id")

  "AuthorityValidatedRegistryEvidence"(e, r) :=
    e."validated-claim" = "RegistryClaim"(r)
    and e."registered-evidence" in r."registry"

  "AuthorityValidatedRegistryEvidence"(e, r)
  implies
  e."validated-claim"."authority-id" = r."registry-authority-id"
  and e."validated-claim"."semantic-id" = r."registry-semantic-id"
$
#+end_export

** Registry trust-claim decision

- Preserve =TrustRootBoundCheckpointWatermarkEvidence= as the weak model and
  counterexample owner.
- Add =PublicationDomainRegistryTrustClaim= as the registry-level authority and
  semantics projection, without domain or Cedar semantic fields.
- Strengthen the existing v1 contract with
  =AuthorityValidatedTrustRootBoundCheckpointWatermarkEvidence=.  Exact claim
  equality binds registry metadata, while the dependent type of
  =registeredEvidence= binds the registry used by the checkpoint evidence.
- Do not introduce V2 and do not use structural equality of the registry
  predicate as a commitment.  The full String/Cedar lifecycle validator remains
  the owner of signature, envelope commitment, and cross-carrier refinement.

** Registry trust-claim checklist

- [X] Audit lineage, replica join, and recovery uses of registry trust-root
  authority and semantic identities.
- [X] Construct two metadata-distinct roots that admit the same registered
  checkpoint evidence through the weak wrapper.
- [X] Define the canonical registry-level trust claim.
- [X] Join an exact validated claim with evidence indexed by the same root
  registry.
- [X] Prove registry authority and semantic identities are preserved.
- [X] Prove either identity mismatch prevents the closed claim from binding.
