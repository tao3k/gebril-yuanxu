* Authority-issued replica-summary provenance

** Replica provenance requirement

Content-aware replica knowledge MUST retain the durable recovery authority and
the proof that this authority issued the exact recovery evidence.  Position
validity and descriptor disposition are necessary but do not establish
issuance.

An n-way summary origin MUST identify the complete authority-issued recovery
event, not only its committed position.  Two authorities or two recovery
receipts at the same position represent distinct accountability obligations and
MUST remain separately representable after every join.

** Valid-but-unissued replica counterexample

The previous knowledge record stored =positionRecoveryValid= but did not store
=authorityIssuedContentAwareRegistryTrustRootRecovery=.  Because Lean structures
have public constructors, the presence of one trusted helper constructor did
not prevent another caller from assembling all fields directly.

=validityOnlyReplicaKnowledgePermitsUnissuedRecovery= constructs a weak
knowledge value from a valid recovery interval while pairing it with an
authority whose =issued= predicate rejects every recovery evidence.  The exact
unissued evidence is nevertheless present in the weak knowledge object.

** Authority-provenance Typst model

#+begin_export typst
$
  "IssuedOrigin"(o) :=
    (o."authority", o."evidence", o."authority-issued-proof")

  "ProvenanceSummary"(s) :=
    (s."minimum-position", s."issued-origins", s."dispositions")

  "JoinProvenance"(l, r)."issued-origins" :=
    l."issued-origins" union r."issued-origins"

  forall o in "JoinProvenance"(l, r)."issued-origins":
    "issued"(o."authority", o."evidence")
    and "DispositionAtJoin"(o, "JoinProvenance"(l, r))
$
#+end_export

** Authority-provenance decision

- Preserve =ValidityOnlyContentAwareReplicaRegistryCheckpointKnowledge= as the
  weak model and explicit unissued-evidence counterexample owner.
- Strengthen =ContentAwareReplicaRegistryCheckpointKnowledge= with the exact
  =recoveryAuthority= and =authorityIssued= proof.  Its public constructor can no
  longer create safe knowledge from validity alone.
- Define =AuthorityIssuedReplicaRecoveryOrigin= as the dependent tuple of
  authority, recovery evidence, and exact acceptance proof.
- Define =AuthorityProvenanceReplicaRegistryCheckpointSummary= over complete
  issued origins rather than =Nat= positions.  The summary still carries origin
  coverage and descriptor dispositions.
- Join provenance summaries by maximum position and union of complete issued
  origins.  Preserve origin and descriptor obligations and prove position/origin
  associative, commutative, and idempotent observation laws.
- Keep the earlier Nat-origin summary as the weaker content-only mathematical
  layer.  Enterprise admission consumes the authority-provenance summary.

** Authority-provenance checklist

- [X] Demonstrate that position validity can coexist with an authority that did
  not issue the recovery evidence.
- [X] Bind safe replica knowledge to the exact recovery authority and issuance
  proof.
- [X] Represent each origin as authority, evidence, and accepted proof.
- [X] Preserve distinct same-position origins as distinct provenance values.
- [X] Construct the singleton provenance summary from issued replica knowledge.
- [X] Preserve every issued origin and descriptor disposition through join.
- [X] Prove provenance-summary position and origin associativity.
- [X] Prove provenance-summary position and origin commutativity.
- [X] Prove provenance-summary position and origin idempotence.
