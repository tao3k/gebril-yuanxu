* Recovered-only descriptor admission witness

** Interval admission requirement

Recovery MUST distinguish a descriptor already registered at the committed
position from a descriptor that first appears before the recovered position.
When a descriptor is absent at the committed position and registered at the
recovered position, the governed lineage MUST expose a concrete adjacent
position inside that interval carrying authority-issued descriptor admission.

Position ordering, final registration, and predecessor descriptor disposition
do not by themselves expose this provenance.  The witness is derived from every
adjacent transition's =successorDisposition= obligation, not invented by the
recovery authority or stored as an unrelated mutable summary field.

** Position-only recovered descriptor counterexample

=positionOnlyRecoveryPermitsUnattributedRecoveredDescriptor= constructs an
identity-stable lineage whose committed registry is empty and whose recovered
registry contains a descriptor.  The recovery positions are ordered, so the
position-only recovery predicate accepts the event while requiring no
descriptor-admission witness.

This does not contradict the strengthened governed lineage.  It demonstrates
that position-only recovery is the weak counterexample owner and cannot support
the enterprise admission claim.

** Interval admission Typst model

#+begin_export typst
$
  "AdmissionAcrossInterval"(d, g, c, r) :=
    exists k:
      c <= k and k < r
      and "AuthorityIssuedAdmission"(d, g(k), g(k + 1))

  "RecoveredOnly"(d, g, c, r) :=
    not "registered"(g(c)."registry", d)
    and "registered"(g(r)."registry", d)

  "GovernedRecovery"(g, c, r) and "RecoveredOnly"(d, g, c, r)
    implies "AdmissionAcrossInterval"(d, g, c, r)
$
#+end_export

** Interval admission decision

- Keep =RegistryTrustRootRecoveryEvidence= as the weak position-only surface and
  counterexample owner.
- Define =DescriptorAdmissionAcrossRegistryInterval= as an existential
  proposition over a concrete adjacent position, rather than as another stored
  recovery record.
- Prove
  =governedLineageRequiresIssuedAdmissionForRecoveredOnlyDescriptor= by
  induction over =committedPosition <= recoveredPosition=.  The reflexive case
  contradicts simultaneous absence and presence; each successor case either
  walks backward through exact preservation or returns the step's
  authority-issued admission.
- Project the same witness from =AuthorityIssuedReplicaRecoveryOrigin=.  The
  canonical provenance summary therefore retains the authority-issued recovery
  owner while the shared governed lineage supplies the descriptor-admission
  step.
- Do not require an admission witness for a descriptor already registered at
  the committed position; its obligation remains preservation-or-issued
  retirement.

- [X] Construct ordered position-only recovery with a recovered-only descriptor
