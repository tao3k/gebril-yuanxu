* Registry recovery and replica content-evidence propagation

** Content-aware recovery and join requirement

Recovery and replica join MUST propagate descriptor disposition evidence, not
only a checkpoint position.  A recovered or joined position is content-safe
only when every descriptor registered at each committed input origin is either
still registered at the selected position or has an authority-validated
retirement at an adjacent transition inside that interval.

Both replica inputs MUST be indexed by the same governed lineage.  Equality or
ordering of their numeric positions does not establish equality of registry
content.

** Position-only recovery and join counterexamples

=positionOnlyRecoveryPermitsSilentRegistryContentLoss= constructs a lineage and
a valid =committedPosition <= recoveredPosition= receipt in which a descriptor
registered at the committed position is absent at the recovered position.

=positionOnlyReplicaJoinIsIndependentOfRegistryContent= constructs two
registries that disagree about a descriptor while the position-only replica
join reports the same maximum checkpoint.  The old Nat projection is therefore
useful for rollback arithmetic but cannot own content admission.

** Content-aware interval Typst model

#+begin_export typst
$
  "IntervalDisposition"(d, g, c, r) :=
    "registered"(g(r)."registry", d)
    or exists k:
      c <= k and k < r
      and "AuthorityValidatedRetirement"(d, g(k), g(k + 1))

  "ContentAwareRecovery"(e, g) :=
    e.c <= e.r
    and forall d in g(e.c)."registry":
      "IntervalDisposition"(d, g, e.c, e.r)

  "ContentAwareJoin"(l, r, j, g) :=
    j."position" = max(l."position", r."position")
    and "DispositionsFromBothOrigins"(l, r, j, g)
$
#+end_export

** Content-aware recovery and join decision

- Define =DescriptorDispositionAcrossRegistryInterval= as final preservation or
  an authority-validated adjacent retirement inside the committed/recovered
  interval.
- Prove by induction that a governed lineage composes adjacent dispositions
  across every ordered interval.  Use
  =contentAwareRecoveryEvidenceOfGovernedLineage= as the canonical constructor;
  callers do not invent a second interval proof language.
- Make =DurableContentAwareRegistryCheckpointRecoveryAuthority= issue the whole
  content-aware receipt and bind its position projection to the existing durable
  recovery authority.
- Derive =ContentAwareReplicaRegistryCheckpointKnowledge= from that issued
  recovery on the same governed-lineage index.
- Require =ContentAwareReplicaRegistryCheckpointJoinEvidence= to carry
  dispositions from both input committed origins to the max-position join.
- Retain the existing Nat recovery and replica join as arithmetic projections
  and counterexample owners, not as full content-admission evidence.

** Content-aware recovery and join checklist

- [X] Construct valid position recovery that loses registry content.
- [X] Construct position-equal replica knowledge independent of registry
  content.
- [X] Define preservation-or-retirement across a recovery interval.
- [X] Prove governed adjacent transitions compose across every ordered
  interval.
- [X] Provide a canonical content-aware recovery constructor.
- [X] Bind content-aware recovery to the durable recovery authority.
- [X] Bind both replica inputs to the same governed lineage.
- [X] Require descriptor dispositions from both committed origins at the joined
  maximum position.
- [X] Preserve the original position join as an exact projection.
