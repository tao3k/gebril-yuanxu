* Gap-separated same-domain rotation

** Temporal rotation requirement

Same-domain rotation MUST remain authority-issued when retirement and admission
occur at different adjacent transitions.  An empty registry generation between
the old and new descriptors MUST NOT erase the relationship visible at the
interval endpoints.

If an old descriptor is registered at the committed position and a distinct new
descriptor with the same =domainIdentity= is registered at the recovered or
summary position, the governed interval MUST expose both:

- an authority-issued retirement step for the old descriptor; and
- an authority-issued admission step for the new descriptor.

The two steps need not be equal.  This permits an intentional quarantine gap
without reducing the rotation to an ungoverned new-domain admission.

** Adjacent-only rotation observation

=sameDomainReplacementRequiresIssuedPredecessorRetirement= correctly owns an
adjacent replacement, but its predecessor-same-domain obligation is vacuous
when the immediate predecessor registry is empty.  Therefore that one-step
theorem cannot by itself justify a gap-separated rotation.

Endpoint registry injectivity supplies the missing bridge.  A distinct
same-domain new descriptor proves that the old descriptor is absent at the
interval end and that the new descriptor was absent at the interval start.
Those facts force the interval retirement branch for the old descriptor and the
interval admission theorem for the new descriptor.

** Temporal rotation Typst model

#+begin_export typst
$
  "SameDomainRotationAcrossInterval"(d_old, d_new, g, c, r) :=
    d_old."domainIdentity" = d_new."domainIdentity"
    and d_old != d_new
    and exists k_retire:
      c <= k_retire and k_retire < r
      and "AuthorityIssuedRetirement"(
        d_old, g(k_retire), g(k_retire + 1),
      )
    and exists k_admit:
      c <= k_admit and k_admit < r
      and "AuthorityIssuedAdmission"(
        d_new, g(k_admit), g(k_admit + 1),
      )
$
#+end_export

** Temporal rotation decision

- Define =DescriptorRetirementAcrossRegistryInterval= as the authority-issued
  retirement existential symmetric to
  =DescriptorAdmissionAcrossRegistryInterval=.
- Define =AuthorityIssuedDomainRotationAcrossRegistryInterval= as same-domain
  distinctness plus both interval witnesses.  Do not require retirement and
  admission to share one adjacent position.
- Prove =governedLineageRequiresIssuedRotationAcrossRegistryInterval= from
  endpoint registrations, interval order, registry identity injectivity, and
  the existing retirement/admission interval theorems.
- Project the paired rotation witness from every covered origin of an
  =AuthorityProvenanceReplicaRegistryCheckpointSummary=.  Because join preserves
  origin coverage and the recovered-head floor, the proof composes through
  N-way summaries.
