* Cedar lifecycle join closure

** Lifecycle join requirement

An authority-bound capability grant is not yet a lifecycle-bound grant.  Before
=use-composition= may consume it, one authority-validated lifecycle binding MUST
join the exact grant to the existing Cedar authorization lifecycle evidence.
The join binds:

- decision identity and commitment;
- accountability identity and responsibility-scope digest;
- active fence token;
- policy snapshot identity and evidence root;
- credential identity and credential generation;
- current authority identity and runtime epoch.

These fields are internal evidence coordinates.  They do not add syntax to the
public =use-composition= macro or permit profiles to mint grants.

** Parallel-model observation and counterexample

The proof audit found three related but distinct type owners:

- =CedarDualEngineAuthorization.AuthorizationSubject=, used by the real grant
  closure;
- =CedarAuthorizationSubjectBridgeModel.AuthorizationSubject=, an independent
  numeric proof model;
- =AuthorizationLifecycleEvidence=, which owns policy epoch, fence, credential,
  authority-path, and separation-of-duty semantics.

Their theorems cannot be conjoined by structural similarity.  No prior theorem
proved that one admitted composition grant denoted the same lifecycle decision.
The counterexample =legacyJoinAcceptsLifecycleUnboundGrant= keeps authority
=17= and epoch =11= equal while replaying different decision, commitment,
fence, policy-evidence, and credential identities.

** Lifecycle join decision

- =CompositionCedarLifecycleJoinClosed= is the only formal bridge from the
  capability-grant closure into =AuthorizationLifecycleEvidence.Closed=.
- The lifecycle model remains owned by its existing module.  Composition
  imports and joins it; it does not copy credential, policy-epoch,
  authority-path, or separation-of-duty semantics.
- The join uses a second generic =AcceptanceAuthority= because the lifecycle
  receipt artifact and its binding are a different evidence type from the
  policy-digest bridge receipt.
- Structural similarity between the independent Cedar proof models is not
  evidence and MUST NOT be used as an implicit coercion.
- The parent owns the admission join.  A =wendao-episteme= profile contributes
  requests only and cannot provide or validate lifecycle grants.

- [X] Lean theorem: closed join rejects active-fence replay.
