* Authority-issued descriptor retirement

** Retirement issuance requirement

A claim-bound retirement record MUST NOT by itself authorize descriptor
deletion.  Safe registry transitions require the exact retirement authority,
the exact claim-bound descriptor evidence, and proof that this authority issued
that descriptor/evidence pair.

The executable registry acceptance authority remains responsible for signature
and commitment validation.  The Lean contract owns the semantic result of that
validation as an =issued= predicate and an issuance proof; it does not fabricate
cryptographic evidence.

** Claim-valid but unissued retirement counterexample

=AuthorityValidatedDescriptorRetirementEvidence= binds predecessor
registration, the canonical predecessor registry claim, and successor
generation.  Those values are all publicly constructible proof inputs.
=claimValidDescriptorRetirementPermitsUnissuedRetirement= constructs that exact
claim-valid evidence together with a retirement authority whose =issued=
predicate rejects every evidence value.

Therefore =Nonempty AuthorityValidatedDescriptorRetirementEvidence= cannot
distinguish an authorized enterprise revocation from locally assembled metadata.

** Retirement issuance Typst model

#+begin_export typst
$
  "ClaimBoundRetirement"(e, p, n, d) :=
    e."descriptor" = d
    and e."registry-claim" = "RegistryClaim"(p."trust-root")
    and e."retirement-generation" = n."generation"

  "AuthorityIssuedRetirement"(a, e, p, n, d) :=
    "ClaimBoundRetirement"(e, p, n, d)
    and a."registry-claim" = "RegistryClaim"(p."trust-root")
    and "issued"(a, d, e)
$
#+end_export

** Retirement issuance decision

- Preserve =AuthorityValidatedDescriptorRetirementEvidence= as the weak
  claim-bound evidence and counterexample owner.
- Add =DescriptorRetirementAuthority= with a canonical predecessor registry
  claim, an =issued descriptor evidence= predicate, and a theorem binding any
  issued evidence to the authority claim.
- Add =AuthorityIssuedDescriptorRetirementEvidence= as the exact weak evidence,
  retirement authority, and issuance proof.
- Change =ContentGovernedRegistryTrustRootTransition= to accept only
  preservation or an inhabited authority-issued retirement wrapper.
- Change registry interval disposition, recovery, and replica propagation to
  carry the issued wrapper.  They may no longer downgrade it to claim-only
  evidence.
- Keep real signature and commitment material at the executable acceptance
  owner; Lean proves the required issuer/evidence relation.

** Retirement issuance checklist

- [X] Construct canonical claim-valid retirement evidence rejected by its
  retirement authority.
- [X] Bind the retirement authority to the canonical predecessor registry
  claim.
- [X] Require issuance for the exact descriptor/evidence pair.
- [X] Prove issued evidence uses the exact authority claim.
- [X] Prove issued retirement retains the exact successor generation.
- [X] Require issued retirement in every content-governed adjacent transition.
- [X] Propagate issued retirement through recovery intervals and replica joins.
