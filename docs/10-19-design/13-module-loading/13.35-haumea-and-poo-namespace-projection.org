* Current contract and reading order

Keep =yuanxu!=, Interface configuration, Gerbil =import= and POO =.get=.
Gerbil owns package installation and compilation; Yuanxu projects selected
owners and their dependency closure into one init-owned =std-make-spec=.
No additional loader, cache layer or compatibility interface is introduced.

Start with modules 01 and 03 for the contracts and user surface, module 05
for graph algorithms, and module 07 for incremental publication. Modules
02, 04, 06 and 09--11 preserve experiments and failure evidence. Modules
08 and 12 describe downstream selection and package boundaries.

Lean operation-count proofs do not establish native throughput. The required
10,000-owner Cases retain 180 seconds and 4 GiB; rejected and incomplete
runs remain visible in the owning measurement documents.
