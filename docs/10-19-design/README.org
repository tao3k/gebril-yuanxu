* Configuration Module System

Read [[file:10-configuration/10.01-index.org][the configuration design map]]. These documents own our proposals,
including lexical dependencies, ordinary data construction, Contract admission,
effective previews and Human-Agent review. They cite independent research under
[[file:../20-29-ecosystem-research/README.org][the research library]].

Keep three statements separate in every decision: what the source establishes,
what we propose, and what an executable control has verified. Preserve rejected
alternatives and reasons when they explain a semantic or UX tradeoff.
