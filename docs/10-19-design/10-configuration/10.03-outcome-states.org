* Issues and Engineering Limitations

References: [[file:../../20-29-ecosystem-research/23-terraform/23.04-schema-plan-and-execution.org][Terraform planning/effects study]] and [[file:../../20-29-ecosystem-research/27-crossplane/27.03-issues-and-observations.org][Crossplane issue records]].
Marks: DESIGN-INFERENCE; NOT-RUNTIME-VALIDATED. The distinctions below are review
requirements. They do not establish that Core or another platform enforces every
transition, and do not convert configuration admission into execution authority.

* Required outcomes

| State | Meaning in our proposed architecture | Does not imply |
|-------+--------------------------------------+-----------------|
| Source-current | Observed configuration/package inputs still match | Remote cloud matches the desired plan |
| Config-admitted | Selected final object satisfies local Contracts | Provider plan or action authorization |
| Provider-planned | Backend accepted/planned against identified inputs | Human/policy approval or readiness |
| Approved | Identified actor/policy permits that specific plan | Any later changed plan is permitted |
| Executed | Requested operation has a recorded outcome | Whole application is converged |
| Ready/converged | Domain/controller observations meet a stated condition | Permanent absence of drift |

Onwards: before implementing Services or cloud automation, define who produces
each observation, its expiry/invalidation, failure propagation and recovery after
partial completion. Do not put those mechanisms into a convenience init macro.
