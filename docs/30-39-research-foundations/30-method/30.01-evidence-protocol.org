* Unit of research

A platform is an independent research subject. Its dossier must explain its own
inputs, language/library use, module/publication mechanism, evaluation model,
Contract/type boundaries, planning/state behavior, execution and user experience
before contributing conclusions to a comparison. Similar product positioning
is not grounds for merging the research records.

Each platform has an Org entrypoint and owns its substantive articles. NixOS,
devenv and Terraform already have separate source/reflection articles; other
dossiers remain initial notes and must not inherit their completion status.
Split a growing dossier along actual research responsibilities, for example:

#+begin_example
20-29-ecosystem-research/
  20-nixos/
    20.01-index.org
    20.02-official-service-module.org
    20.04-discovery-and-dependency-injection.org
    20.07-issues-and-evidence.org
    20.08-real-service-composition-and-design-decisions.org
#+end_example

This is a growth pattern, not a requirement to create empty files. A completed
source audit or case reproduction may need several modules of its own. Keep a
clear source owner for each finding rather than duplicating prose across files.

* Required content of an independent dossier

1. Research questions and scope: distinguish language, library, engine, hosted
   service and provider. Identify maintainers and ordinary-user responsibilities.
2. Real configuration corpus: upstream file paths, repository/release identity,
   complete required inputs and the expected consumer/output. Excerpts retain
   enough enclosing structure to compare the same data types and meanings.
3. Mechanism analysis: publication/import, argument passing, dependency discovery,
   merging, identity, caching/invalidation and phase boundaries. Separate facts
   from architecture inferred from documentation.
4. Types/Contracts and diagnostics: what is checked, what remains unknown, how
   requirements survive composition, and where the user sees an error.
5. Operational semantics: plan, state, locking, effects, readiness, partial failure
   and versioned rollout, where the platform owns these responsibilities.
6. Issue records: one issue per subsection or document with source, report date,
   affected version, observation, status/check date and reproduction status.
   A closed historical report is not evidence of a current unfixed bug.
7. User-experience comparison: ordinary values, inherited extension and contribution
   maintenance. Count repeated definitions and concepts the author must know.
8. Reflection: strengths worth retaining, weaknesses, tradeoffs, counterexamples
   to our preferred design, and what Scheme/POO does not solve.
9. Conclusion and Onwards: bounded findings, unanswered questions, design candidates,
   and concrete next observations that could support or refute them.
10. Human-Agent horizon: identify whether the inspected source explicitly studies
    Agent-assisted authoring (EXPLICIT), does not discuss it in the inspected
    material (NOT-DISCUSSED), or has not been checked (UNKNOWN). Record the material
    actually inspected. Revisit authoring-cost assumptions without dismissing
    semantic results or inferring an author's intent from publication date.

* Claim and status vocabulary

| Claim class | Meaning | Insufficient substitute |
|-------------+---------+-------------------------|
| Documented | Specific primary reference describes behavior | Product positioning or search snippet |
| Source-observed | Identified source files/revision support the finding | A guessed implementation mechanism |
| Issue-reported | Reporter describes a case on an identified version | Confirmed universal platform behavior |
| Reproduced | Executable case and recorded environment/result | Merely copying a reproduction into a document |
| Design inference | Our proposed response or interpretation | Already implemented or formally proved behavior |
| Open | Evidence or design decision still missing | A placeholder marked complete |

Each platform updates its own evidence status; completion is not inherited from
another platform. Record inaccessible references and unknown versions. A citation
can motivate investigation but does not replace inspecting its actual contents.
Do not manufacture issue IDs for products whose relevant issue corpus has not
been examined; official documented limits may suffice for the specific claim.

* Issues and Engineering Limitations: mandatory article section

Each substantive research article must contain this heading. It may link to its
platform's detailed issue owner instead of duplicating records. Keep an Issues
topic even when no relevant GitHub defect has been verified; say what is known
from documentation and what remains unexamined. A References link without an
interpretation is insufficient.

Every record carries:

- Stable local ID and an original GitHub issue/discussion, official source or
  article URL; identify the source kind. An external article is a research lead
  until its particular claims are checked against primary evidence.
- Report/publication date, affected version/revision and observation date. Use
  UNKNOWN or NOT-REFRESHED explicitly instead of inferring a current status.
- Marks for DOCUMENTED-LIMITATION, ISSUE-REPORTED, MAINTAINER-EXPLAINED,
  CONFIRMED-DEFECT, HISTORICAL-RESOLVED, DESIGN-TRADEOFF or DESIGN-INFERENCE, as
  supported. These marks are not synonyms and can be combined.
- Upstream state and our reproduction state separately. CONFIRMED-DEFECT needs
  confirmation evidence; a GitHub bug label or an unhappy report alone is not it.
- Owning layer, actual user/engineering cost, and the design question or negative
  control motivated by the observation. Preserve counterevidence and later fixes.

An article is not complete merely because it contains this section. Initial
dossiers may still have an unreviewed issue corpus; label that gap rather than
inventing a report. A design risk in our proposal is not an upstream defect.

* Matched cases and review

AWS and Guestbook provide shared questions but do not impose identical execution
engines. Compare final input/resource semantics, then explicitly identify what
cannot be made equivalent. Keep deployed behavior, local admission and static
source inspection as separate evidence. Preserve failed and stale controls.

Research completion means the important questions have defensible answers or
explicit unresolved boundaries. It does not mean a directory exists. Runtime,
macro or Loader implementation remains deferred while the design is reviewed.


* Evidence proportionality: documentation and source before installation

Installing every reviewed platform is not the default research workflow.
Start from official user documentation, configuration examples, versioned source,
upstream tests, Issues and relevant fixing PRs. An explicitly documented interface
or inspected merge implementation can establish that bounded finding without
installing the platform locally. Source study is a substantive result, not an
incomplete installation task.

Only select a local reproduction when a specific unanswered claim requires it:
version-dependent diagnostics, disputed behavior, performance, runtime/provider
interaction or an ambiguous source path. State the question and the smallest
necessary runtime before fetching dependencies. Do not fetch all providers or
an entire platform suite to inspect a configuration API. Performance and actual
effect claims still require execution evidence; source analysis must not be
reclassified as a locally reproduced failure or speed advantage.

For every comparison record what the reference guarantees, what its code does,
what a report alleges, and what we infer separately. Absence from a manual does
not prove lack of capability. A proposed stronger framework guarantee needs a
mechanism and counterexample, but not an indiscriminate installation campaign.

On 2026-10-17 the broad Gitea/PostgreSQL runtime campaign was stopped in favor
of this source-first scope. One existing-host NixOS evaluation completed and
remains a narrow local observation; Pulumi engine execution did not occur.
Temporary package acquisition is not platform validation. No three-platform
result, maintenance-cost ranking or new superiority conclusion is established.
