* Interpretation from initial reading

Reflection: may a resource be admitted but not ready? Yes. May an operation finish
successfully while the whole Scenario is not converged? Yes. What evidence changes
either judgment? Provider/controller observations, not module loading success.

Conclusion: keep one-shot Ops, long-lived Services and reconciliation separate.
No Services implementation is authorized by this research. Reuse a controller or
provider engine where it supplies the required lifecycle semantics.
