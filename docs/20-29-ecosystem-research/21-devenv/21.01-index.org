* Research purpose

Study how a maintained Nix module ecosystem gives developers small configuration
interfaces while owning package, environment and process details. This is a
module-system case, not a claim that Nix syntax is better or worse than Scheme.

Read [[file:21.02-composition-and-postgres.org][composition, PostgreSQL source, issue evidence and reflection]]. The scope is
one real service plus documented composition, not a completed audit of every
devenv module, evaluator or cache. Source/document access date: 2026-10-17.
Runtime, service readiness and performance have not been validated here.

* Issues and Engineering Limitations

The [[file:21.02-composition-and-postgres.org][study's issue register]] owns DV-01 (PostgreSQL PGHOST regression report) and
DV-02 (configuration merge versus operational relationships). Marks distinguish
the historical user report from current source and our design inferences.

* Conclusion

The research target is lifecycle and compatibility work hidden by ordinary values.
Reusing a rich host language does not remove that work. Compare these findings
with [[file:../20-nixos/20.02-official-service-module.org][NixOS's official service module]] and [[file:../22-flox/22.01-index.org][Flox's environment composition]].
