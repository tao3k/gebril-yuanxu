* Identifier rules

An area owns ten categories; a category owns numbered items in the form AC.ID.
The category prefix matches its area, for example category 20 in area 20–29.
Use 20.01 for the NixOS reading index, with subsequent items for substantive
research. Every item has its identifier in the filename, title and DOCUMENT_ID.
Area README files and this routing index are navigation, not numbered research
items. Category 31 may grow a 31.03 reading index without renumbering its papers.

Allocate the next unused identifier; do not renumber existing items to change
reading order. Keep reading order in the category index. New substantial subjects
receive their own available category or a newly allocated area, rather than a
third directory layer under a crowded platform category. Reserve unused area
and category numbers until a real owner exists. Do not generate empty documents.

NixOS, devenv and Flox remain independent research categories even when they use
related Nix mechanisms. A paper owns its assumptions and conclusions independently
of a platform. Design items cite research owners; research evidence is not silently
upgraded to an implemented design or an executed validation.

* Migration boundary

The documents maintained in this docs tree now use Johnny.Decimal. Relative Org
links in the existing Core documentation were rewritten against original targets.
The old docs/design and docs/research trees have no duplicate document owners.

The former top-level Core design tree is fully migrated into categories 11–13,
including its supporting assets and design specimens. There is no forwarding
copy under design/. Historical proposals and qualification limits retain their
status; moving a document does not establish an implementation result.

[[file:../module-system/design/README.org][Engineering/style documents]] remain their separate normative owner. This
migration does not renumber those RFCs or change their authority. No implemented
module source moves with this documentation work.
